What Happens After You Dán a Video Reddit Link

ReditGrab looks up the post's public media information and checks for a Reddit-hosted video. If it finds one, you get a playable preview first — not an instant download. You can confirm it's the clip you meant, check the resolution, and use the unmute button to hear whether it has sound.

The quality menu lists the versions Reddit has for that post, with the highest one selected by default. When you're happy, Tải xuống saves the MP4 through your browser.

Video Reddit URL Formats That Work

Sao chép liên kết bài đăng, không phải subreddit. Các dạng liên kết sau đều được hỗ trợ:

  • reddit.com/r/subreddit/comments/… — liên kết bài đăng tiêu chuẩn
  • old.reddit.com/r/subreddit/comments/…
  • redd.it/… — liên kết rút gọn từ menu chia sẻ của ứng dụng, được tự động mở rộng
  • v.redd.it/… — liên kết video trực tiếp

Bài đăng chéo hoạt động nếu video gốc được lưu trữ trên Reddit. Photo galleries belong in the Trình tải ảnh Reddit, profile pictures in the Trình tải ảnh đại diện Reddit, and videos that are only embeds from YouTube or Streamable aren't hosted by Reddit. Not sure? See Choosing the Right Reddit Link.

Tải xuống Video Reddit With Sound

Nếu bài đăng gốc có âm thanh thì thường là có. Reddit lưu video và âm thanh thành các tệp riêng thay vì một tệp đã ghép sẵn. That's why some tools give you a silent clip — they grabbed only the picture. ReditGrab requests both tracks when they exist and combines them into one MP4 where it can.

A saved video can still be silent for two different reasons. The first is that it never had audio: a GIF uploaded as video, a screen recording with the mic off, or a clip with the sound stripped. The second is rarer — the post has an audio track but it couldn't be merged on that attempt. The result card shows which case you're in before you download. Full explanation: Why Video Reddits Have No Sound.

Video Reddit Quality and MP4 Format

Bạn nhận được tệp MP4. Độ phân giải là phiên bản Reddit đã lưu cho video. ReditGrab không phóng to, nén lại hoặc thêm watermark. Reddit often keeps several smaller versions of the same video, and each one appears in the quality menu.

A low-resolution upload only has low-resolution versions — no downloader can sharpen it. Pick the highest version if you plan to keep or edit the clip, and a lower one if storage or mobile data is tight. Understanding Video Reddit Độ phân giảis goes deeper. Reddit Save Button vs Tải xuốnging a File

Reddit's own Save button bookmarks the post inside your Reddit account — it doesn't put a video file on your device, and you lose access if the post is deleted. Tải xuốnging through ReditGrab saves the actual MP4 file locally, independent of Reddit.
