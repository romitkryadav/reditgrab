Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

What We Collect

ReditGrab does not require accounts or collect names, passwords, or Reddit login credentials. The service receives the public URL you submit so it can locate the requested media.

How Downloads Work

Public media URLs and download requests may pass through the configured Cloudflare Worker or a supported media service. Media is streamed to your browser and is not intentionally stored as a personal library by ReditGrab.

Cookies and Local Storage

The site may use browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. The tools do not need tracking cookies to perform downloads.

Third-Party Services

Reddit, Cloudflare, and media delivery providers may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms. ReditGrab does not control those third-party systems.

Your Responsibility

Only submit public Reddit URLs and download content you are permitted to use. Do not provide passwords, private tokens, or other sensitive information through the tools.
