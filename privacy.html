Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What We Collect

ReditGrab does not require an account and does not ask for your Reddit password or login credentials. When you use a tool, ReditGrab receives the Reddit post URL or profile name you submit so it can look for supported, publicly accessible media. Hosting, network, and processing providers may also receive technical request data such as your IP address, request time, and browser details.

How Downloads Work

Submitted post URLs or profile names are sent to ReditGrab’s processing service. Depending on the tool and current configuration, requests may pass through Cloudflare Workers and other hosting or delivery providers, and media may be served by Reddit or a supported image host. ReditGrab does not provide user accounts or a personal media library. Providers may process or retain request data under their own policies; ReditGrab does not control their logs or retention.

Cookies and Browser Storage

ReditGrab stores your theme preference and analytics choice in your browser’s local storage. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you accept optional analytics. After you accept, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as _ga and receive information about site use, browser and device details, approximate location, and traffic source. If you reject analytics, it stays blocked on future visits. If you change an accepted choice to reject, ReditGrab updates consent and removes its first-party Google Analytics cookies. Use the control below to change your choice. Other browser extensions and services on your device have their own storage behavior.

Analytics

If you accept optional analytics, Google Analytics 4 measures site visits and interactions, including pages viewed, browser and device details, approximate location, and traffic source. Analytics is optional, and rejecting it does not affect the downloader. See Google's description of Analytics data collection.

Third-Party Services

Reddit or a supported image host may provide the media. Cloudflare may process requests for media lookup or delivery. The contact form sends the name, email address, and message you enter through FormSubmit to contact@reditgrab.com. Google Analytics is loaded only after you accept optional analytics. Each provider handles data under its own terms. FormSubmit says it retains form submissions for 30 days; see its privacy information for current details. ReditGrab does not control provider systems or retention.

Contact Form

If you send a message, your name, email address, and message go to FormSubmit for delivery to contact@reditgrab.com. FormSubmit may process technical request information to provide and protect the form. Avoid including passwords, private tokens, financial details, or other sensitive information. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days; its own policy governs its handling.

Your Responsibility

Use the tools only with publicly accessible Reddit content that you are permitted to access and use. Do not submit passwords, private tokens, or other sensitive information. ReditGrab is not designed to bypass Reddit access controls. You are responsible for respecting applicable law, Reddit’s terms, and the rights of content owners.

Privacy Questions

Questions about this policy or data handling? Contact ReditGrab through the Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

ReditGrab may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently revised.
