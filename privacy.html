Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

What We Collect

ReditGrab does not require user accounts and does not ask for names, passwords, Reddit login credentials, or private access tokens. When you use a downloader, the service receives the public Reddit URL you submit so it can attempt to locate and process the requested media. Depending on the infrastructure used to handle a request, standard technical request information may also be processed by service providers such as Cloudflare.

How Downloads Work

Public Reddit media URLs and download requests may pass through the configured Cloudflare Worker or another supported media service used by the downloader. Media is processed or streamed as part of the request and sent to your browser. ReditGrab does not intentionally maintain downloaded Reddit videos as a personal media library.

Cookies and Local Storage

ReditGrab may use your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as the selected light or dark theme. The downloader does not require tracking cookies to perform a media download. Browser extensions, privacy tools, or other services on your device may have their own storage or tracking behavior outside ReditGrab's control.

Third-Party Services

ReditGrab relies on third-party infrastructure and media platforms to provide parts of the service. Reddit may provide the public post or media source, while Cloudflare and other configured service providers may handle network requests, processing, or delivery. These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms, and ReditGrab does not control their systems or data practices.

Your Responsibility

Only submit public Reddit URLs and download content that you are permitted to access and use. Do not submit passwords, private tokens, authentication credentials, or other sensitive information through ReditGrab's tools. ReditGrab is designed to process publicly accessible content and does not provide a way to bypass Reddit's access controls.

Privacy Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by ReditGrab, please contact us through the Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

ReditGrab may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, infrastructure, or applicable requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently revised.
