Reddit video downloader compatibility evidence

This page records what was checked in a small manual sample. It reports observed tool behavior, not a promise that every Reddit post or browser will work.

Manual compatibility check

Checked October 26, 2026 in a desktop Chrome session using three public Reddit share links supplied for this check.

3 post lookups3 returned quality choices1 MP4 download inspected

What we observed

  • All three links resolved to a video result and displayed 720p, 480p, 360p, 270p, and 220p choices.
  • One 720p MP4 download completed. Its file was about 30.1 MB and its MP4 container included both a video track and an audio track.
  • A separate preview loaded a 1280 × 720 video stream and a separate audio stream with a reported duration of about 20 seconds.

Limits of this check

Three links are a small sample, not a representative success rate. Results can change when Reddit changes its media delivery or when posts are removed or restricted. The audio track was detected in the downloaded file, but we did not verify audible playback or synchronization across devices.

The sample links are not published here. Private, deleted, login-required, and externally hosted videos were not part of this check.
