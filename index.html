How to Download Reddit Videos as MP4

Copy the link, paste it above, preview and save. Here's what happens at each step.

1

Copy the Post Link

Open the specific Reddit post. In the app tap Share then Copy link. In a browser, copy the address bar. Copy the post link — not the subreddit or homepage URL.

2

Paste & Get Video

Paste the link into the box above and click Get Video. ReditGrab looks up the post's public media and checks for a Reddit-hosted video stream.

3

Preview & Download

Confirm the clip in the player, unmute to check for sound, pick a quality from the menu, and click Download to save the MP4 to your device.

What Happens After You Paste a Reddit Video Link

ReditGrab looks up the post's public media information and checks for a Reddit-hosted video. If it finds one, you get a playable preview first — not an instant download. You can confirm it's the clip you meant, check the resolution, and use the unmute button to hear whether it has sound.

The quality menu lists the versions Reddit has for that post, with the highest one selected by default. When you're happy, Download saves the MP4 through your browser.

Reddit Video URL Formats That Work

Copy the link to the post, not the subreddit. These link shapes all work:

  • reddit.com/r/subreddit/comments/… — standard post links
  • old.reddit.com/r/subreddit/comments/…
  • redd.it/… — short links from the app's share sheet, expanded automatically
  • v.redd.it/… — direct video links

Crossposts work if the original is a Reddit-hosted video. Photo galleries belong in the Reddit Image Downloader, profile pictures in the Reddit DP Downloader, and videos that are only embeds from YouTube or Streamable aren't hosted by Reddit. Not sure? See Choosing the Right Reddit Link.

Download Reddit Video With Sound

If the original post has audio, usually yes. Reddit stores its videos as separate video and audio files rather than one combined file. That's why some tools give you a silent clip — they grabbed only the picture. ReditGrab requests both tracks when they exist and combines them into one MP4 where it can.

A saved video can still be silent for two different reasons. The first is that it never had audio: a GIF uploaded as video, a screen recording with the mic off, or a clip with the sound stripped. The second is rarer — the post has an audio track but it couldn't be merged on that attempt. The result card shows which case you're in before you download. Full explanation: Why Reddit Videos Have No Sound.

Reddit Video Quality and MP4 Format

You get an MP4. The resolution is whatever Reddit stored for the upload. ReditGrab doesn't upscale, re-compress, or add a watermark. Reddit often keeps several smaller versions of the same video, and each one appears in the quality menu.

A low-resolution upload only has low-resolution versions — no downloader can sharpen it. Pick the highest version if you plan to keep or edit the clip, and a lower one if storage or mobile data is tight. Understanding Reddit Video Resolutions goes deeper. Save Reddit Videos on iPhone, Android or PC

Phone

Copy the link in the Reddit app, open reditgrab.com in your mobile browser, paste, tap Get Video, then Download. On Android the file goes to your Downloads folder. On iPhone it usually lands in the Files app under Downloads — open it there, tap Share, then Save Video to add it to Photos. Step-by-step guides: iPhone · Android.

Computer

The flow is the same in any browser on Windows or Mac. Copy the address-bar link, paste, preview, download. The file goes to your browser's download folder. No extension is required. See why you don't need an app.

Reddit Video Downloader Not Working — Fixes

Most failures have a specific cause and a clear fix.

What you see Likely reason What to try
Invalid link error You copied a subreddit, profile or search URL, or the link got cut off Open the exact post and copy again from Share → Copy link
Post not found Deleted by the poster or removed by moderators since you first saw it Open the link in a private browser window. If it doesn't load there, the video is gone
Private or login-required ReditGrab only reads posts anyone can view Same private-window test. If Reddit asks you to log in, ReditGrab can't fetch it either
No video found The post is a photo, gallery, text post or an external embed Use the Image Downloader for photos. For embeds, use a downloader for the original site
Preview won't play Your browser blocked loading media directly from Reddit's servers Switch to the proxy playback option on the player or reload the player. Download may still work
Video has no sound Silent at the source, or the audio couldn't be merged Check the result card, then try once more. See the sound section above
Download does nothing Browser blocking the download, a blocking extension, or full storage Allow downloads for reditgrab.com, try the Backup button, then try another browser
Worked last week, fails now Reddit sometimes changes how it serves media Try again later. If it keeps failing, send the link through Contact

No downloader works for every link every day, because it depends on how Reddit serves media at that moment.

How ReditGrab Handles Your Data

ReditGrab doesn't ask you to sign in and only handles posts anyone can view. It doesn't intentionally store the videos you download on its servers. Like any online tool, it has to see the link you paste — the Privacy page has the details. Never enter your Reddit password into a downloader.

The videos belong to the people who made them. Saving one to watch offline is different from reposting it, so credit the creator and subreddit if you share it. Reddit Posts This Tool Cannot Download

  • Private, quarantined or login-only posts
  • Deleted posts
  • Videos embedded from other sites (YouTube, Streamable, etc.)
  • NSFW posts that require an account to view

Reddit Save Button vs Downloading a File

Reddit's own Save button bookmarks the post inside your Reddit account — it doesn't put a video file on your device, and you lose access if the post is deleted. Downloading through ReditGrab saves the actual MP4 file locally, independent of Reddit.
