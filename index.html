How to Download Reddit Videos

Downloading videos from Reddit is quick and easy with ReditGrab. Follow these three simple steps.

1

Copy the Reddit Post Link

Open any public Reddit video post on the app or website. Tap Share and select "Copy Link", or copy the URL from your browser address bar.

2

Paste & Fetch the Video

Paste the copied Reddit link into the input box above and click "Get Video". Our Cloudflare Worker will instantly detect and load the media.

3

Preview & Download

Preview the video in the player, pick your preferred quality, and click Download to save the MP4 directly to your device.

Supported Reddit Video Link Formats

ReditGrab recognizes every common Reddit URL shape. Standard reddit.com/r/… post links, old.reddit.com URLs, the shortened redd.it links generated by the mobile share sheet, and direct v.redd.it stream addresses all work. Short links are silently resolved to their full post URL before anything is fetched, so you don't need to manually expand them first.

The one requirement is that the post must be publicly readable — not behind a login, not inside a private or quarantined community. The Preview Step Exists for a Reason

Once you paste a link and click "Get Video", the tool fetches the post metadata and locates the video stream. Instead of triggering a download immediately, it loads a playable preview first. That way you can scrub through the clip, verify it's the right video, and check the resolution before committing to a download. You also choose the quality level yourself — the highest available is selected by default, but other options appear if Reddit has encoded multiple resolutions for that post. Read more in Understanding Reddit Video Resolutions. The whole process runs in your browser with nothing to install — see why you don't need an app for this.

How Reddit Stores Video (and Why It Matters)

Reddit uses DASH (Dynamic Adaptive Streaming over HTTP) for videos hosted at v.redd.it. Under DASH, the video track and audio track are stored as completely separate files on Reddit's CDN — they're not packaged together at the source. This is why Reddit videos sometimes appear silent when played in third-party apps: the player only grabbed the video track. ReditGrab requests both tracks when they exist and, where the download path supports it, merges them into a single MP4 before the file reaches your device. Interestingly, this same split-track behaviour is why posts that look like GIFs — looping, silent clips — are actually stored as videos: read Reddit Video vs GIF for the full story on how that conversion works.

Silent Videos — What Actually Causes Them

Two different situations produce a video without sound, and they have different causes. First, some posts are genuinely silent: the uploader converted a GIF or screen recording that had no audio to begin with, so there is nothing to merge. Second, a small number of posts have a separate audio track that the current download path can't merge cleanly — in that case you'll see the video-only file. The tool indicates which situation applies so you know whether missing audio is a source limitation or a processing one. For a full explanation, see Why Reddit Videos Have No Sound. If you're on an iPhone and running into this, the iPhone-specific download guide covers exactly why the Reddit app's own save button tends to drop the audio track.

Resolution and Output Format

The file you download is an MP4. The resolution comes directly from what Reddit encoded when the post was uploaded — ReditGrab doesn't re-encode, upscale, compress further, or add a watermark. If Reddit has multiple quality tiers for a post (commonly 360p, 720p, and 1080p), all available options are shown in the quality selector so you can pick the one that suits your storage or bandwidth needs. Learn how to pick the right tier in Understanding Reddit Video Resolutions, or follow the full step-by-step download guide. On Android, the file lands straight in your Downloads folder — the Android guide covers where to find it and how to move it to your gallery.

When a Link Fails to Load

Most failures come down to one of three things: the post has been deleted or removed by moderators, the subreddit is private or quarantined, or the post contains a link to an external site rather than a Reddit-hosted video. In all three cases the tool shows you a specific error rather than a generic "something went wrong" message. Deleted posts are the most common — if a video was live when you saw it but won't load now, it was likely removed in the time between.
