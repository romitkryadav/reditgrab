How to Download Reddit Videos

Downloading videos from Reddit is quick and easy with ReditGrab. Follow these three simple steps.

1

Copy the Reddit Post Link

Open any public Reddit video post on the app or website. Tap Share and select "Copy Link", or copy the URL from your browser address bar.

2

Paste & Fetch the Video

Paste the copied Reddit link into the input box above and click "Get Video". Our Cloudflare Worker will instantly detect and load the media.

3

Preview & Download

Preview the video in the player, pick your preferred quality, and click Download to save the MP4 directly to your device.

Which Reddit Posts This Works On

This tool reads public Reddit post data only. It works with standard reddit.com links, old.reddit.com links, mobile app share links, and redd.it short links (these redirect to the full post before the video is fetched). It does not work on posts inside private communities, quarantined subreddits, or anything that requires you to be logged in to view — the tool has no way to authenticate as you, and it isn't built to get around that. If you're trying to save a photo or gallery instead of a video, use the Reddit Image Downloader. For a profile picture specifically, use the Reddit DP Downloader.

What Happens After You Paste a Link

The tool reads the public post data behind the link and looks for a Reddit-hosted video stream. If it finds one, you'll get a preview so you can confirm it's the right clip before downloading anything. From there you can save the video, or save the audio track on its own if the post has one. Nothing downloads automatically — you choose what to save after seeing the preview.

Video Quality and File Format

The downloader uses the highest available quality it can retrieve from the public Reddit media source. The available resolution depends on the original video uploaded to Reddit. This tool doesn't upscale or fabricate a higher resolution than the source has. Output is MP4.

Why Some Reddit Videos Have No Sound

Reddit stores video hosted at v.redd.it using DASH streaming, which keeps the video track and the audio track as two separate files. Some posts genuinely have no audio at all — this is common for clips converted from silent GIFs. When a post does have an audio track, the downloader can combine it with the video for a single MP4 when the active download path supports merging.

What This Tool Won't Do

It only works on public content. It can't reach videos from private subreddits, posts requiring you to be logged in, quarantined communities, or anything that's been deleted or removed — there's no way around Reddit's own access controls here, and there shouldn't be. If a link doesn't resolve, that's usually why.

Use it On Phone or on Desktop

The steps are the same either way: open Reddit, find the post, copy the link (the Share button on mobile, the address bar on desktop), and paste it above. No app or extension to install on either platform.
