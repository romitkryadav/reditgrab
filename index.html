How to Descargar Reddit Videos as MP4

Copy the link, paste it above, preview and save. Here's what happens at each step.

1

Copia el enlace de la publicación

Abre la publicación concreta de Reddit. En la aplicación, pulsa Compartir y después Copiar enlace. En el navegador, copia la barra de direcciones. Copia el enlace de la publicación, no el subreddit ni la página principal.

2

Paste & Obtener vídeo

Paste the link into the box above and click Obtener vídeo. ReditGrab looks up the post's public media and checks for a Reddit-hosted video stream.

3

Preview & Descargar

Confirm the clip in the player, unmute to check for sound, pick a quality from the menu, and click Descargar to save the MP4 to your device.

Qué ocurre después de pegar un enlace de vídeo de Reddit

ReditGrab looks up the post's public media information and checks for a Reddit-hosted video. If it finds one, you get a playable preview first — not an instant download. You can confirm it's the clip you meant, check the resolution, and use the unmute button to hear whether it has sound.

The quality menu lists the versions Reddit has for that post, with the highest one selected by default. When you're happy, Descargar saves the MP4 through your browser.

Formatos de enlaces de vídeo de Reddit compatibles

Copia el enlace de la publicación, no el subreddit. Estos formatos son compatibles:

  • reddit.com/r/subreddit/comments/… — enlaces normales de publicaciones
  • old.reddit.com/r/subreddit/comments/…
  • redd.it/… — enlaces cortos de la aplicación que se amplían automáticamente
  • v.redd.it/… — enlaces directos al vídeo

Crossposts work if the original is a Reddit-hosted video. Photo galleries belong in the Reddit Descargar imágenes, profile pictures in the Reddit Foto de perfil, and videos that are only embeds from YouTube or Streamable aren't hosted by Reddit. Not sure? See Choosing the Right Reddit Link.

Descargar Reddit Video With Sound

If the original post has audio, usually yes. Reddit stores its videos as separate video and audio files rather than one combined file. That's why some tools give you a silent clip — they grabbed only the picture. ReditGrab requests both tracks when they exist and combines them into one MP4 where it can.

A saved video can still be silent for two different reasons. The first is that it never had audio: a GIF uploaded as video, a screen recording with the mic off, or a clip with the sound stripped. The second is rarer — the post has an audio track but it couldn't be merged on that attempt. The result card shows which case you're in before you download. Full explanation: Why Reddit Videos Have No Sound.

Calidad de los vídeos de Reddit y formato MP4

You get an MP4. The resolution is whatever Reddit stored for the upload. ReditGrab doesn't upscale, re-compress, or add a watermark. Reddit often keeps several smaller versions of the same video, and each one appears in the quality menu.

A low-resolution upload only has low-resolution versions — no downloader can sharpen it. Pick the highest version if you plan to keep or edit the clip, and a lower one if storage or mobile data is tight. Understanding Reddit Video Resolucións goes deeper. Guardar vídeos de Reddit en iMóvil, Android u ordenador

Móvil

Copy the link in the Reddit app, open reditgrab.com in your mobile browser, paste, tap Obtener vídeo, then Descargar. On Android the file goes to your Descargars folder. On iMóvil it usually lands in the Files app under Descargars — open it there, tap Share, then Save Video to add it to Photos. Step-by-step guides: iMóvil · Android.

Ordenador

The flow is the same in any browser on Windows or Mac. Copy the address-bar link, paste, preview, download. The file goes to your browser's download folder. No extension is required. See why you don't need an app.

Descargador de vídeos de Reddit Not Working — Fixes

La mayoría de los errores tienen una causa concreta y una solución sencilla.

Qué ves Causa probable Qué puedes probar
Error de enlace no válido You copied a subreddit, profile or search URL, or the link got cut off Open the exact post and copy again from Share → Copy link
Publicación no encontrada Deleted by the poster or removed by moderators since you first saw it Open the link in a private browser window. If it doesn't load there, the video is gone
Privada o requiere iniciar sesión ReditGrab only reads posts anyone can view Same private-window test. If Reddit asks you to log in, ReditGrab can't fetch it either
No se ha encontrado ningún vídeo The post is a photo, gallery, text post or an external embed Use the Descargar imágenes for photos. For embeds, use a downloader for the original site
La vista previa no se reproduce Your browser blocked loading media directly from Reddit's servers Switch to the proxy playback option on the player or reload the player. Descargar may still work
El vídeo no tiene sonido Silent at the source, or the audio couldn't be merged Check the result card, then try once more. See the sound section above
Descargar does nothing Browser blocking the download, a blocking extension, or full storage Allow downloads for reditgrab.com, try the Copia de seguridad button, then try another browser
Funcionaba la semana pasada y ahora falla Reddit sometimes changes how it serves media Try again later. If it keeps failing, send the link through Contacto

Ningún descargador funciona con todos los enlaces todos los días, porque depende de cómo Reddit sirve el contenido multimedia en ese momento.

Cómo gestiona ReditGrab tus datos

ReditGrab no te pide iniciar sesión y solo procesa publicaciones que cualquiera puede ver. No almacena intencionadamente los vídeos que descargas en sus servidores. Like any online tool, it has to see the link you paste — the Privacidad page has the details. Never enter your Reddit password into a downloader.

Uso legal y responsable de los vídeos de Reddit

Los vídeos pertenecen a las personas que los crearon. Guardar uno para verlo sin conexión es diferente de volver a publicarlo, así que acredita al creador y al subreddit si lo compartes. The rules depend on where you live and what you do with the file — the legal overview covers the basics. ReditGrab is not affiliated with Reddit.

Reddit Posts This Tool Cannot Descargar

  • Publicaciones privadas, en cuarentena o que requieren iniciar sesión
  • Publicaciones eliminadas
  • Vídeos incrustados desde otros sitios (YouTube, Streamable, etc.)
  • Publicaciones NSFW que requieren una cuenta para verse

Reddit Save Button vs Descargaring a File

Reddit's own Save button bookmarks the post inside your Reddit account — it doesn't put a video file on your device, and you lose access if the post is deleted. Descargaring through ReditGrab saves the actual MP4 file locally, independent of Reddit.
